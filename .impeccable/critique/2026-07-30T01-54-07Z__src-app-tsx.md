## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Authored, but only moderately differentiated. The “Caderno Clinico Pastel” system and the Brazil-specific clinical-study copy give Locus a calm, credible identity. The landing-page composition is nevertheless familiar: headline, card grid, testimonials, pricing. Its strongest product artifact is not visible in the first viewport.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json src/App.tsx` returned `[]` (exit 0): zero findings, locations, or false positives. Browser inspection and overlay injection were unavailable because no browser was available in this runtime.

## Overall Impression

Warm, audience-literate, and visually coherent. It needs one undeniable proof of the product before it asks a student to compare plans.

## What's Working

- The copy understands the context: rounds, handoffs, WhatsApp PDFs, and offline hospital study establish trust.
- The pastel clinical-note token system is cohesive and restrained.
- Pricing exposes first-cycle price, renewal cadence, monthly equivalent, cancellation, and the seven-day guarantee.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Header/navigation is not rendered.** `App.tsx` imports only Hero and Footer; Header is never mounted. **Why:** visitors lose orientation and access to product paths. **Fix:** render a compact sales header with one primary CTA and only conversion-relevant controls. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`.
- **[P1] The primary claim has no immediate proof.** **Why:** referral traffic needs evidence that the product is more than polished copy. **Fix:** show one real clinical study artifact beside/below the hero—annotated question feedback, a chapter, and the offline state. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable bolder`.
- **[P1] Pricing is cognitively expensive.** Near-identical feature lists force reconciliation of promotion, renewal, cadence, and monthly equivalent. **Fix:** provide a recommendation rationale tied to semester/budget and standardize renewal information. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`.
- **[P2] The email preview is unreachable and brittle.** `isEmailModalOpen` is never made true; a failed template fetch can leave an empty modal. **Fix:** remove it from the sales page or make it reachable with load/error/retry, Escape, focus management, and an accessible close label. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`.
- **[P2] Accessibility semantics need work.** FAQ controls need accordion semantics; focus visibility and icon naming are insufficient. **Fix:** add `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, robust `:focus-visible` rings, and programmatic state announcements. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan (first-timer):** “Começar agora” jumps to pricing before proof; repeated “Testar na plataforma” controls do not explain that the landing will be replaced by a demo; support is limited to FAQs.
- **Riley (stress tester):** email-template fetch failure is not recovered; external checkout has no in-page selection/confirmation; offline scope is not clearly bounded.
- **Casey (mobile):** reaching pricing takes long vertical scanning through five feature and three pricing cards; the absent header provides no anchored escape route.

## Minor Observations

- Header expects a `calculator` tab that App cannot represent.
- Pricing is mounted on both routes with the same `planos` id.
- Generic initials and five-star testimonials undercut otherwise specific copy.
- The pulsing green dot implies a status without explanation.

## Questions to Consider

- Should plan guidance map to semester length, budget certainty, and study pressure rather than “Most popular”?
- What should a referred student know before leaving for checkout?
